الأسئلة الشائعة

Salon FAQs

Everything you need to know before your Beauty Wave appointment.

How far in advance should I book?

For color, bridal and long appointments, we recommend booking a few days ahead. Shorter services may have same-day availability.

Are the listed prices inclusive of VAT?

Yes. The prices shown on the menu are inclusive of VAT. Complex color or extra-length work may be quoted after consultation.

Can I request a consultation before a color service?

Absolutely. Share your desired result and current hair details in the booking notes and the team can advise before confirming the service.

How do I confirm my appointment?

Submit the booking form and WhatsApp will open with your details ready to send. The salon can then confirm the appointment directly.
